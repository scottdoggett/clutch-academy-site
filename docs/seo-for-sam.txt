WHAT IS SEO, IN ONE SENTENCE?
-----------------------------
SEO is the work that gets your business to show up when people search
for what you sell. For Clutch, that's "manual driving lessons toronto"
and similar phrases — in Google, in Bing, and increasingly in AI
assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity.


THE BIG CHANGE THIS SUMMER
==========================

Your original site was one long scrolling page. It looked great, but
it gave Google exactly ONE page to rank, competing for every search
term at once. That's a real handicap.

We rebuilt it as a proper multi-page site. It now has nine pages,
each one aimed at something specific:

  - Home
  - Lessons overview (all four packages compared)
  - Individual Manual Lesson
  - Manual Foundations (3 lessons)
  - Complete Manual Confidence (5 lessons)
  - Group Manual Lessons
  - About
  - FAQ
  - Contact

Why this matters: someone searching "group manual lessons toronto"
now lands on a page that is *entirely about* group lessons, instead of
a homepage that mentions them halfway down. Google rewards that, and
so do customers — they book more often when the page answers the exact
thing they searched for.

It also gives you four proper destinations for Google Ads, instead of
sending every ad to the same homepage.

IMPORTANT: the new site is BUILT but not yet switched on. Your live
site is still the old one. The new version is deployed separately so
you can review it before we flip the switch. Nothing changes for your
customers until we do.


WHAT WE'VE DONE FOR YOU (THE HIGHLIGHTS)
========================================

1. MADE THE SITE READABLE TO GOOGLE AND AI ASSISTANTS
   Most modern websites are tricky for search engines to read. Yours
   is built so Google, Bing, ChatGPT, Claude, and the rest can see the
   full content — services, prices, FAQ, contact info — even if they
   don't run any JavaScript. This is the single biggest technical
   thing we did, and it's completely invisible to you.

2. CONNECTED YOUR WEBSITE TO YOUR GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE
   When someone Googles "Clutch Academy", your Maps listing and your
   website are officially linked. Google understands they're the same
   business. This helps you appear in the "local 3-pack" (the map
   results at the top of local searches) and gives AI assistants
   confidence to recommend you.

3. LISTED YOUR BUSINESS INFO IN A FORMAT GOOGLE LOVES
   Behind the scenes we tell Google things like:
   - You're a driving school in Toronto
   - Your packages and prices: $109 single lesson (75 min), $299 for
     three lessons, $469 for five, $219 for a 2.5-hour group lesson
   - Sam Anthony is the founder and instructor
   - Your phone, email, Instagram, Facebook, and Google Business
     Profile
   - The areas you serve: Toronto, Etobicoke, North York,
     Scarborough, East York, Mississauga, Vaughan

   This is why your prices can show up directly in search results.

4. PUBLISHED YOUR FAQ IN A SPECIAL FORMAT
   Your FAQ answers are marked up so Google can show them as
   expandable questions right in the search results. More space on
   the page for you, less for competitors.

5. OPENED THE DOOR TO AI ASSISTANTS DELIBERATELY
   Most sites either block AI crawlers or ignore them. We explicitly
   welcome sixteen of them — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google's AI,
   Apple's, and others — and we publish a plain-language summary file
   just for them. When someone asks ChatGPT "where can I learn stick
   shift in Toronto", we want you in that answer.

   This was a deliberate business decision. If you ever want to
   reverse it, say so — it's a one-file change.

6. COVERED THE WORDS PEOPLE ACTUALLY USE
   Not everyone says "manual". Plenty of people search "stick shift"
   or "standard". The site uses all three deliberately, and there's an
   FAQ entry explaining they're the same thing — which catches the
   people who genuinely aren't sure.

7. MADE IT FAST AND ACCESSIBLE
   The site scores 100/100 on Google's accessibility and SEO audits
   across every page. Accessibility is a ranking factor and, more
   importantly, it means the site works for everyone.

WHAT TO TRACK (EVERY COUPLE OF WEEKS)
=====================================

Don't obsess. Fifteen minutes, twice a month.

1. GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE INSIGHTS
   How many people found you, called, or asked for directions. This
   is your realest number.

2. YOUR REVIEW COUNT AND AVERAGE
   Still the highest-leverage lever you have. Keep asking.

3. SEARCH CONSOLE (once it's turned on — see next-steps)
   Which searches show your site, and where you rank for them.

4. WHERE BOOKINGS ACTUALLY COME FROM
   Every "Book" button on the site is tagged, so we can tell whether
   bookings come from the homepage, a package page, the FAQ, or an
   ad. That tells us what's working.

WHAT NOT TO TRACK: your own Google searches. Google personalises
results based on your history and location, so searching for yourself
tells you almost nothing. Use the Search Console numbers instead.
